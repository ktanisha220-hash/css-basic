TCS ION NQT-Cognitive

TCS exam will be held after the training

The TCS ION NQT-Cognitive is an online assessment that evaluates a candidate's cognitive abilities, problem-solving skills, and logical reasoning. It is designed to assess the candidate's aptitude in various areas such as numerical reasoning, verbal reasoning, and analytical thinking.


  • The results for your exam will directly affect your placement opportunities. So, it is important to prepare well for the exam and give your best performance

    • It helps candidates to identify their strengths and weaknesses in various areas of cognitive abilities.
